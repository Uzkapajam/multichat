MultiChat

Twitch, YouTube and TikTok live chat in one window - made for streamers.

TwitchYouTubeTikTok

What it does

  • Shows live chat from Twitch, YouTube and TikTok in a single feed, with an icon showing where each message came from.
  • Lets the streamer write to chat and moderate it (delete messages, timeouts, bans) on their own channels.
  • Shows stream info such as uptime, viewer count, title and category.
  • Provides a chat overlay for OBS on the streamer's own computer or local network.

Google / YouTube sign-in

MultiChat is a desktop application. When you choose "Sign in with YouTube", it uses Google sign-in to read your live stream's chat, post messages you write, and moderate your live chat. It only accesses YouTube on your behalf while the app is running, and only for these features.

Privacy policy

MultiChat is an independent project and is not affiliated with Twitch, Google, YouTube or TikTok.
