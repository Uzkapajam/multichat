Privacy Policy

MultiChat - last updated 15 November 2026

MultiChat is a desktop application that shows Twitch, YouTube and TikTok live chat in one window. This policy explains what data it uses and how.

No servers, no tracking

MultiChat runs entirely on your own computer. The developer does not operate any server that receives your data. MultiChat does not collect analytics, does not track you and does not sell or share any data.

Google / YouTube data

If you sign in with Google, MultiChat requests access to your YouTube account (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl) and uses it only to:

  • find your active live stream and read its live chat,
  • post chat messages that you type in MultiChat,
  • moderate your live chat when you choose to (delete messages, time out or ban users),
  • show your channel name and picture, and your stream's title and viewer count.

This data is requested directly from Google's YouTube API by the app on your computer and is shown to you. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party, and it is not stored except for the sign-in token described below.

Sign-in tokens

Sign-in tokens for Google and Twitch are stored only on your computer, encrypted with your operating system's secure storage (Windows DPAPI). You can remove them at any time by signing out in MultiChat's settings, or revoke access to Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Twitch and TikTok

Twitch chat is read and sent directly with Twitch's services using your own Twitch sign-in. TikTok live chat is read-only, using a public connection library and its signing service; MultiChat does not sign in to TikTok and does not send your TikTok account details anywhere. Emote images are loaded from 7TV, BTTV and FrankerFaceZ.

Limited Use

MultiChat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. MultiChat uses the YouTube API Services; see also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page. For questions, contact the developer using the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
